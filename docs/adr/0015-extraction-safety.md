# ADR 0015 — Extraction safety boundary

Status: Accepted for the private Linux staging subset; store publication remains future work.

Extraction must occur only after trusted artifact verification, into a private staging directory through no-follow directory-relative filesystem operations. Archive inspection alone cannot authorize extraction or installation. Until symlink, hardlink, special-file, mode-bit and filesystem race policies are proven, reject links, special files, setuid/setgid/sticky modes, unknown extension/xattr records and unsafe or ambiguous paths. Reject absolute paths, parent components, duplicate normalized names and file/directory collisions. Do not preserve archive ownership or execute scripts. A future extractor must validate its destination, handle hostile concurrent changes without escaping the staging root, enforce resource limits, and test cleanup and failures. The extractor stages only verified cached artifacts and does not publish or activate them.

Read-only archive inspection now rejects absolute and parent paths, duplicate normalized paths, file/directory collisions, unsafe mode bits, links, special files and extension records. Linux staging validates the cached artifact and its authorization before and after copying into a private 0700 directory, using openat2 beneath the held directory descriptor and exclusive no-follow file creation. It rejects all archive links, special files, unsupported extensions and unsafe mode bits, and creates files as 0600 without ownership, xattrs or capabilities. An accessor opens regular staged files through the held descriptor rather than exposing a pathname.

Staging requires Linux openat2 and a root-owned safe `/tmp`; lack of support fails closed. The temporary tree is not durable or immutable and cleanup is best effort. Same-UID processes may still mutate its contents, and the tests do not prove absence of every race. Future store adoption must reverify content and establish its own durable publication and concurrency boundary; staged bytes are not installable.
